Engineering & Design

Team Lead, UriCare

Engineering Capstone · University of Calgary

Leading a 5-person team to develop a smartphone-connected, non-invasive bladder ultrasound system for people with MS.

<2.7% blinded-test error 88.75 patient SUS
UltrasoundSignal ProcessingMobile Health
Tap for details ↓

From need to prototype

Started with interviews with 3 MS patients, translating challenges around bladder monitoring, manual tracking, and clinician visibility into engineering requirements.

How it works

A 7.5 MHz ultrasound transducer sends pulses through the bladder and captures echoes from its walls. Time-of-flight signal processing estimates bladder dimensions and volume, then sends the result to a smartphone app for real-time monitoring, tracking, and alerts.

My role

Led project planning, sponsor coordination, testing, and validation while contributing to the electrical system and integration across hardware, software, and clinical needs.

Biosignals + Human-Centred Design

Sleep Detection Eye Mask

Project Management Lead · BMEN 468

A wearable proof of concept using eye movement signals to detect REM sleep.

EOGAgCl ElectrodesPrototyping

The idea

Designed a sleep-detection eye mask using electrooculography (EOG) to capture eye movements and blinks associated with REM sleep.

How it works

AgCl electrodes measure potential differences generated by eye movement, allowing the system to detect changes without cameras or external motion tracking.

Designed for a human

Developed the prototype around electrode placement, skin-contact materials, comfort, and clinical relevance, not just whether the signal could be captured.

Enterprise Data + Visualization

Indigenous Engagement Analytics

Digital Engineering · Veren

Built the company's first centralized Indigenous engagement analytics system, turning fragmented enterprise data into an interactive reporting experience.

Power BIDenodoSQL

The problem

Engagement information was spread across fragmented data sources, making reporting manual and limiting visibility across the organization.

The build

Built a centralized analytics system in Denodo and Power BI, including an interactive map showing First Nations alongside company assets to make relationships easier to explore and understand.

The iteration

Used closed-loop stakeholder feedback throughout development, continuously testing, gathering input, and refining the system around how teams actually worked.

The result

By delivery, the internally built platform had evolved to a level comparable to vendor solutions, while being tailored to the company's own data and workflows.
